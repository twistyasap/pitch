01

The project

Numbers that warn us.

I used numbers from Fortnite players.

My goal: find the players who are losing interest, before they leave the game.

Simple idea: when a player plays less and less, it is a warning sign.

02

The context

Players leave in silence.

Fortnite is a very popular online game. Millions of people play it.

But every day, some players stop playing — and they don’t say goodbye.

The problem: the company sees it too late. Fewer players = less money.

03

How I did it

Three simple steps.

  1. Step 1Collect

    I got public stats from 1,435 real players.

  2. Step 2Look

    I studied three numbers: minutes played, matches and wins.

  3. Step 3Group

    A computer program put the players into groups.

Machine learning = the computer looks at a lot of data and finds patterns by itself.
