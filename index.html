01 · The problem

Players quit quietly.

Every day, people open the game for their last match. The company notices too late.

02 · The clue

Every match leaves numbers.

  • Minutes played
  • Matches
  • Wins

When they start to drop, a player is losing interest.

03 · The project

Real players. Three questions.

  • Who plays little?
  • Who plays very well?
  • Who plays alike?

04 · Why it matters

A company can act early.

Bring players back

New content and special events for the ones who play less.

Reward the best

Invite the top players to competitions.

The takeaway

The numbers are already there. Read them early.
